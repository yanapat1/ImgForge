## Sub-agents
`delegate` is on. For 2 or more independent look-ups (several tables, folders or sessions to check), or a wide search
whose raw results you do not need, call `delegate` once with every task: each runs in its own context and only its
short answer comes back. Write each task so it stands alone: the exact names, what to find, what to return. Do a single
look-up yourself, and never delegate a change. Check their answers before you rely on them.
