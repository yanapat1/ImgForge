- **Augment** (`aug`, sparkle icon in the activity bar; not in an embeddings session) previews an **Albumentations** recipe on this session's images before anyone trains with it.
- **Left: the recipe.** *Start from* a preset (Day outdoor, Night / low light, Bad weather, New camera (generic), Cheap / low-res camera, Motion & vibration, Indoor lighting, Geometry (YOLO-like)), then each step is a card: on / off, its **chance**, its own settings (ranges are low–high, e.g. brightness −0.2…0.2), ↑ ↓ to reorder, × to remove, and the eye (**solo**) to preview only that step at full chance. **+ Add augmentation** lists 37 transforms by group (Light & colour, Weather & scene, Camera & sensor, Blur & motion, Geometry with boxes following, Occlusion) with what each simulates and a small example on one of the images. **Apply**: every step by its chance, one step per image, or some (n) steps per image. **Strength** scales every step at once (0 = no change, 2 = twice as strong).
- **Right: Preview.** Rows of images: the original, then the variants with the boxes drawn in class colours; each variant names the steps that ran, and a red badge counts boxes lost (less than 30 % of a box stayed inside). Images: random, one per class, or the user's selection (from the map or gallery); 3 to 12 images, 2 to 8 variants; **Again** draws new ones. Click a variant: a big view with a wipe between original and augmented, Space held shows the original, and Full size.
- **Recipe ▾**: save under a name (per user, per session), open a saved one, import an Albumentations JSON (`A.save` / `A.to_dict`; transforms this tab does not have are listed and left out), export as Albumentations JSON, Python code (an `A.Compose` with YOLO boxes) or YAML.
- **You**: `augment_catalog` lists the transforms, what they simulate and their default arguments, and the presets; `set_augment_recipe` puts a recipe in the tab and opens it (the user sees it on their images). Choose steps for the change the user describes: a new camera = colour temperature, tone curve, sharpness, lens distortion, JPEG and noise; night = darker, gamma, ISO noise; rain / fog = weather steps; motion = motion blur. Keep chances moderate (0.2–0.5) so most images stay close to the real ones, and say what each step stands for.
- The version is albumentations 2.0.8 (MIT). Geometry steps move the boxes too; colour and weather steps do not.
