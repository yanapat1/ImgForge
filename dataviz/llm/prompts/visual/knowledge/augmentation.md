- **Augment** (`aug`, sparkle icon in the activity bar; not in an embeddings session) previews an **Albumentations** recipe on this session's images before anyone trains with it.
- **Left: the recipe.** *Start from* a preset (Day outdoor, Night / low light, Bad weather, New camera (generic), Cheap / low-res camera, Motion & vibration, Indoor lighting, Geometry (YOLO-like)), then each step is a card: on / off, its **chance**, its own settings (ranges are low–high, e.g. brightness −0.2…0.2), ↑ ↓ to reorder, × to remove, and the eye (**solo**) to preview only that step at full chance. **+ Add augmentation** lists 37 transforms by group (Light & colour, Weather & scene, Camera & sensor, Blur & motion, Geometry with boxes following, Occlusion) with what each simulates and a small example on one of the images. **Apply**: every step by its chance, one step per image, or some (n) steps per image. **Strength** scales every step at once (0 = no change, 2 = twice as strong).
- **Right: Preview.** Rows of images: the original, then the variants with the boxes drawn in class colours; an augmented variant has an accent outline, an **Augmented** badge and the steps that ran as chips, one where nothing ran is dimmed and marked **Unchanged**, and a red badge counts boxes lost (less than 30 % of a box stayed inside). Images: random, one per class, or the user's selection (from the map or gallery); 3 to 12 images, 2 to 8 variants; **Again** draws new ones. **Show on the map** draws about 60 images' augmented boxes on the Explore map as open diamonds in their class colour: each crop is embedded with the map's own feature and placed at the weighted position of its 10 nearest dataset boxes (2D maps of deep, colour or HOG features; not a geometry map). A diamond inside its class's cluster is a realistic variant, one in another class's cluster or in empty space means a step changes the object too much; the chip at the bottom hides them. Click a variant: a large viewer in the page (no new tab): hold the mouse on the image and drag left / right to wipe between the original (left) and the augmented image (right), ← → move the line, Space held shows the original; a sharp version loads by itself.
- **Boxes** (the right panel's second view): **Check boxes** runs the recipe on 50–500 images of every class (1–4 variants each) as a job and counts what happens to the labels: **lost** (less than 30 % of a box left in the image), **too small** (short side under 4–16 px once resized to the train size, 416–1280), **newly at the edge** (cut by a crop or a shift); per step (in the variants it ran on) and per class, with warnings and examples that lost boxes (click one: the same compare viewer, the boxes on the original side that are gone on the augmented side). Use it before training: a crop or shift that loses many boxes of a class needs a lower chance or strength.
- **Coverage** (third view): *does the augmented data cover images from another situation or a new camera?* **Compare against**: images uploaded here (Upload images…, or drop them; up to 1000, kept with the session), a Data Center folder (its images and subfolders, up to 2000; a streamed S3 folder must be downloaded first), or **another visualizer session** the user can open (its images, up to 2000 evenly spread, read from where that session reads them, local or S3). **Features**: quick (colour + edges, CPU, seconds) or deep (the session's DINOv2 / CLIP features; slow without a GPU). **Check coverage** runs a job: up to 1000 dataset images, 1–4 augmented variants each, and the new images are embedded (each part cached, so a recipe change only redoes the augmented part). The report:
  - **Coverage before → after**: the share of new images as close to the dataset (then dataset + augmented) as the dataset's own images are to each other (95th percentile, measured on the dataset alone, so augmenting cannot lower the bar).
  - **Fréchet distance** and **tell-apart AUC** (0.5 = a classifier cannot tell the sets apart), before → after; **unreal variants**: augmented images like neither set (too strong).
  - A map of the three sets (dataset grey, augmented in the accent colour, new images, uncovered ones ringed red); **which steps helped**; the images **still not covered** (click one: beside its nearest dataset and augmented image); **what they differ in** (darker / brighter, contrast, blur, colour strength, colour temperature, noise), each with **+ step** to add it to the recipe; **Compare with a saved recipe** puts a second recipe beside it (A/B).
  - **Find a recipe for these images** (next to Check coverage) searches as a job: starting from the user's recipe it tries each step (first those for what the new images differ in), keeps the one that raises their coverage most, tunes its chance, and repeats (at most 4 steps, while a step gains 2 points or more; augmented images like neither set count against it). It shows each added step with the coverage after it, and **Use this recipe** puts it in place. With no step that helps, it says so: then real images from the new place are needed.
  - **Strength curve** runs the recipe at strengths 0.25× to 2× against the same images: coverage and unreal variants at each, the best strength (most coverage without too many unreal variants) and **Use strength N×**.
  - Coverage measures similar *appearance* in feature space; it does not prove a model will be accurate there. Real labelled images from the new place stay the best test.
- **Model** (fourth view): pick a detection model (Settings › Models) and **Test the model**: it predicts 50–300 images of every class with each step of the recipe at full chance, then under **Mixed** every step together at full chance and the whole recipe as it runs (each step by its chance), and scores each against the labels as the step moved them (mAP50, P, R). The table says per step the drop from the clean images and whether the model *handles it* (keeps 95 % of its clean mAP50) or *struggles*. Train with the steps it struggles on; the ones it handles add little.
- **Recipe ▾**: save under a name (per user, per session), open a saved one, import an Albumentations JSON (`A.save` / `A.to_dict`; transforms this tab does not have are listed and left out), export as Albumentations JSON, Python code (an `A.Compose` with YOLO boxes), YAML, or **for Ultralytics training** (a script passing the steps as `augmentations=` to `YOLO(...).train`, on top of YOLO's own augmentation). **Make an augmented copy in the Data Center…** writes every image of the session through the recipe (1–10 variants each, the originals too if ticked) into a new managed bucket, with YOLO labels that follow the boxes (lost boxes left out), classes.txt and recipe.json; it runs as a job.
- **You**: `augment_coverage` reads the newest coverage report (numbers, verdict, gaps with the step to add); explain it and propose a better recipe with `set_augment_recipe`, then ask the user to run the check again. `augment_catalog` lists the transforms, what they simulate and their default arguments, and the presets; `set_augment_recipe` puts a recipe in the tab and opens it (the user sees it on their images). Choose steps for the change the user describes: a new camera = colour temperature, tone curve, sharpness, lens distortion, JPEG and noise; night = darker, gamma, ISO noise; rain / fog = weather steps; motion = motion blur. Keep chances moderate (0.2–0.5) so most images stay close to the real ones, and say what each step stands for.
- The version is albumentations 2.0.8 (MIT). Geometry steps move the boxes too; colour and weather steps do not.
